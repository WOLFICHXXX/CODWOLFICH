Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Data we receive

  • Sign-in: your ID and display name from Telegram, Google or Discord. We never receive or store passwords.
  • Alliance data you enter: in-game nickname, power, merits, marches, troops, heroes and skills, prime time, alliance role, RoW plan.
  • Screenshots: are recognized in your browser; the images are not sent to the server.
  • Technical data: hosting service logs (e.g. request time) — to run and protect the service.

2. Why

So you can sign in to your account, see your alliance and member cards, receive notifications you turned on (in Telegram), and so the service works reliably.

3. Where it is stored

Data is stored on Cloudflare servers (Workers, D1, KV); a backup mirror runs on Yandex Cloud. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages.

4. Who can see it

A member's card is visible to members of their alliance; the creator and officers can edit alliance data. We do not sell data or share it with third parties for advertising.

5. Discord

The Discord app requests only the identify scope (your Discord ID and username). We do not receive your messages, servers or friends list.

6. Retention

As long as the account or alliance exists. Deleted cards and alliances are removed from the database.

7. Your rights

You can edit or delete your card, leave an alliance, unlink a sign-in method and ask us to delete your account and all related data — contact @WOLFICH_COD_BOT on Telegram.

8. Children

The service is not intended for children under 13.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
